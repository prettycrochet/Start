Home decor:
Cozy things made by hand

Baskets, pillows, coasters and wall decor – crochet projects that bring warmth to your home and are useful along the way.

Why home decor?

Crochet you see every day

For me, home decor is the most rewarding crochet category of all. The projects are usually worked flat or in the round, forgive small irregularities, and you see them every day afterwards – unlike a scarf that spends eight months in the closet.

On top of that: many home decor projects grow quickly. A coaster is done in half an hour, a small basket in one evening. That makes them perfect for anyone who has just learned the basic stitches and needs a sense of achievement before tackling something bigger.

If terms like “single crochet” or “double crochet” don't mean anything to you yet, it's best to start with the Crochet Basics – after that, every project on this page is very doable.

Supplies

Materials for home decor

🧶

Sturdy cotton

For coasters, potholders and anything that gets washed. Holds its shape and is more heat-resistant than acrylic.

🪢

T-shirt yarn / rope

Chunky yarn for baskets and planter covers – stands up on its own and grows incredibly fast.

🌾

Jute & natural yarn

For a rustic boho look on wall wreaths and plant hangers.

🪝

Larger hooks

With T-shirt yarn you'll need 6–10 mm. A comfortable grip matters more here than usual.

⭕

Metal or wooden rings

The base for wall wreaths and plant hangers – available in many diameters.

🔖

Stitch markers

Essential for round projects, so you don't lose track of the start of the round.

Basic techniques

What you should know

01

Crocheting flat in the round

The basis for coasters, baskets and rugs: even increases in every round make sure the circle stays flat and doesn't ruffle or cup.

02

Back loop only

The trick behind the ribbed look of baskets: if you only insert your hook into the back loop, a fine horizontal texture appears and the wall turns up neatly.

03

From the base upwards

Once the circle is big enough, stop increasing and keep crocheting straight – this automatically becomes the wall of the basket.

04

Assembling pieces

Flowers, leaves and appliqués are crocheted separately, then arranged and sewn on – that's how wreaths and embellished pillows are made.

My home decor tips

Check round pieces regularly

If your circle cups upwards, you're missing increases. If the edge ruffles, there are too many. Lay it flat on the table every now and then.

Match colors to the room

Muted natural tones look calm in almost any interior style. Add color with a single accent piece rather than with every piece at once.

Crochet tightly for baskets

The tighter the stitches, the better the basket holds its shape. When in doubt, use a hook one size smaller than the yarn label suggests.
