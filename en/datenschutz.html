This website does not set cookies and does not use any analytics, tracking or advertising services. No content from external servers is embedded. If you send me an email, I process your email address and the content of your message in order to answer your request. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR if your request relates to a contract, and otherwise Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR; my legitimate interest lies in answering your request.

You can delete the data at any time via “Clear part” or in your browser settings.
