Oktoberfest Crochet: Pretzel, Gingerbread Heart & Wiesn Decor – Plus a Free Mini Pretzel Pattern

By PrettyCrochet · Published on October 3, 2026 · Reading time: approx. 6 minutes

In short

Wiesn decor is great fun to crochet: pretzel, gingerbread heart, beer mug coasters, edelweiss and a blue-and-white bunting are made almost entirely from single crochet and are ideal for beginners. The colors: blue and white, plus pretzel brown, cream and a strong heart red. Further down you'll find free patterns for a mini pretzel and a pennant.

Oktoberfest 2026 runs until Sunday, October 4 – high time for a little Wiesn spirit on the hook. I have to admit I like the festival better from a distance, but the pretzel, the gingerbread heart and the blue-and-white diamond pattern are simply perfect crochet motifs. They're small, quick to make and still look lovely after the festival – on the fall table, on a bag or as a small gift. Here are the ideas and two little patterns to crochet along.

1. Which projects suit Oktoberfest?

MotifWhy it worksLevel
🥨 PretzelA simple tube of single crochet that is shaped into a pretzelBeginner
❤️ Gingerbread heartA flat heart, the white icing script is embroidered onBeginner+

Tip: Start with the pretzel. It only needs the magic ring and single crochet – you can learn both in the crochet basics.

2. Which colors suit Oktoberfest?

  • White & cream
  • Bavarian blue
  • Muted blue
  • Pretzel brown
  • Gingerbread brown
  • Heart red

The color swatches are a guide – compare them with real yarn in the shop.

The classic Bavarian blue with white is bold. If you prefer something calmer, choose a muted blue and cream instead of pure white – then the decor looks modern and still fits into your home after the festival.

3. Crocheted Wiesn decor

A little Wiesn corner only needs three things: a bunting in blue and white, a few pretzels to hang up and two beer mug coasters. All of it is done in one cozy evening. You crochet the coasters like a flat circle – in every round add as many increases as you had stitches in the first round. How that works is explained in the crochet basics.

If you want to keep track of your rounds, the stitch counter helps: it counts rows and stitches and remembers where you stopped.

4. The diamond pattern: blue and white for advanced crocheters

The Bavarian diamond pattern consists of blue and white diamonds in alternation. It is crocheted with two colors, for example in tapestry crochet, where you carry the unused yarn inside your stitches. That is more of an advanced technique – for beginners it is easier to make single-color pennants and alternate blue and white.

Pattern: Mini pretzel

Size
approx. 3 in (8 cm) wide
Time
about 1.5–2 hours
Level
Beginner
Yarn
Cotton in caramel or pretzel brown (approx. 20 g), a little cream for the salt
Tools
2.5–3 mm hook, toy stuffing, stitch marker, tapestry needle, optional key ring

Abbreviations (US terms)

MR = magic ring · sc = single crochet · dec = single crochet 2 together · ch = chain. All abbreviations are listed in the abbreviations guide.

Work in continuous rounds. Mark the first stitch of each round with a stitch marker.

Tube (brown or caramel)

  1. MR, 6 sc (6)
  2. Round 2 until the desired length: 6 sc around (6)

Keep going until the tube is about 10 in (25 cm) long (the number of rounds depends on your yarn, so measure). Stuff the tube lightly in sections as you go – push the stuffing in with a blunt pencil. Cut the yarn, weave it through the last 6 stitches, pull tight and weave in the end.

Shaping the pretzel

  1. Lay the tube in a U shape.
  2. Cross the two ends over each other twice, like a twist.
  3. Fold the ends down onto the curve of the U and sew them in place with a few stitches.
  4. Add one or two stitches at the crossings as well so the shape holds.

Salt

With cream yarn, spread 8 to 10 small stitches evenly over the pretzel. Weave in the ends well.

As a bag charm: crochet a loop of 10 to 12 ch at the top of the pretzel and attach the key ring. You'll find more on the topic in the post Crochet bag charms.

Pattern: Pennant for the blue-and-white bunting

Size
approx. 2 × 1.5 in (5 × 4 cm), depending on the yarn
Time
about 15 minutes per pennant
Level
Beginner
Yarn
Cotton in blue and in white or cream
  1. Ch 17.
  2. Sc in the 2nd ch from hook and in each ch across (16)
  3. Ch 1, turn. Dec, 12 sc, dec (14)
  4. Ch 1, turn. Dec, 10 sc, dec (12)
  5. Ch 1, turn. Dec, 8 sc, dec (10)
  6. Ch 1, turn. Dec, 6 sc, dec (8)
  7. Ch 1, turn. Dec, 4 sc, dec (6)
  8. Ch 1, turn. Dec, 2 sc, dec (4)
  9. Ch 1, turn. Dec, dec (2)
  10. Ch 1, turn. Dec (1)

Cut the yarn and weave in the end. The turning chain does not count as a stitch. Make the pennants alternately in blue and white and sew them to a cord or a chain of crochet chains.

Frequently asked questions about Oktoberfest crochet

01

When is Oktoberfest 2026?

The 191st Oktoberfest runs from Saturday, September 19, to Sunday, October 4, 2026 on the Theresienwiese in Munich. Crocheted Wiesn decor also works for fall festivals, theme parties and as a small gift.

02

Which Oktoberfest motifs can you crochet?

Popular choices are the pretzel, gingerbread heart, beer mug coasters, edelweiss and bunting in the blue-and-white diamond pattern. Most of them are made from simple single crochet and suit beginners.

03

Which colors suit Oktoberfest decor?

Typical are Bavarian blue and white, plus pretzel and gingerbread brown, cream and a strong heart red. For a calmer look, choose a muted blue instead of sky blue.

04

How do you crochet a pretzel?

You crochet a thin tube with 6 single crochet per round, stuff it lightly, lay it in a U, cross the ends twice and sew them to the curve. Finally you embroider small grains of salt in cream yarn.

05

Which yarn works for crocheted pretzels?

Smooth cotton yarn in caramel or brown with a 2.5–3 mm hook works best. It holds its shape and the stuffing does not show through.
